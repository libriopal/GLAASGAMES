# Privacy Policy — GLAAS 4D

**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

GLAAS 4D does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app does not do

- It does not request the `INTERNET` permission. The app is technically
  incapable of sending anything anywhere; this is enforced by Android, not
  promised by us.
- It requests no permissions of any kind.
- It has no account, no login, and no identifier.
- It does not use an advertising ID.
- It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no
  third-party SDKs that collect data.
- It shows no ads and offers no purchases.

## What the app stores

Nothing leaves your device, and nothing is backed up. The daily puzzle is
derived from the calendar date by arithmetic performed on your device, so there
is no server to fetch it from and no record of you having played it.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it ever had.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
