Trace up to six connected dice; the faces under them score as a Farkle hand. Cells leave the hand as they score and nothing replaces them until the turn ends — so the hand shrinks, connectivity dies, and the risk climbs. Hand size 16 is swept against the real tabletop farkle curve (2.3 / 7.8 / 15.8 / 27.8 / 44.3 / 66.8 % by dice remaining), landing RMS 4.7 under efficient play. Risk is endogenous: the same board at the same step is 28.1% lethal to one policy and 44.4% to another.
