## Prompt

A real-money skill-based sweepstakes game defends its legality partly on SKILL PREDOMINANCE — that outcomes are determined primarily by player decisions rather than chance, under the dominant-factor test. The same codebase contains an Opportunity Weight Controller that detects when a player is behind and boosts their draw probabilities to create comeback opportunities. Name the strongest legal or fairness objection this combination creates.

## Auditor response

The strongest objection is **deceptive trade practices (fraud)**. The "Opportunity Weight Controller" introduces algorithmic manipulation of chance that directly invalidates the "skill predominance" defense. By programmatically boosting probabilities to engineer "comeback opportunities," the operator is substituting player skill with a hidden, non-skill-based mechanism designed to steer outcomes, rendering the game's legal classification factually false.

This bites when the controller’s probability adjustments are significant enough to prevent player skill from being the "dominant factor" in determining the outcome under the applicable legal test.
