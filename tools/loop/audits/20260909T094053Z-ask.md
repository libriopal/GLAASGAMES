## Auditor response

Yes, this resolves the gradient objection. However, the plan remains unsafe due to **proxy-task overfitting (Goodhart's Law)**. You are optimizing structural loci against a surrogate metric (the "inference value") rather than the ultimate objective (the oracle suite).

The plan fails if the verifier's gradient is not perfectly monotonic with respect to the oracle suite's success. If the verifier rewards specific mathematical patterns that do not directly translate to the functional capabilities required by the oracle, the breeding process will optimize for the verifier's specific artifacts. This creates "degenerate" structural genomes that maximize the inference value while remaining incapable of passing the admission constraint, effectively decoupling the breeding driver from the actual goal.
