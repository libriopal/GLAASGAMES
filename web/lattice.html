GLAAS · Lattice

Take a cell each turn. Energy flows along hidden links — until you show the answer.

How to play

Each cell feeds one hidden neighbour. You score the face of the cell you feed — not the one you take.
  1. Tap a cell to take it.
  2. Watch which cell lights up: that is where its energy went.
  3. Cells in the same area tend to flow the same way. Use that.
